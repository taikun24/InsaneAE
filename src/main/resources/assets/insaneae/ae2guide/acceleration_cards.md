Higher-tier <ItemLink id="ae2:speed_card" />s: Turbo ×8, Overclock ×64, Hypersonic ×512 and Warp ×4096.
They multiply the machine's own speed instead of counting as more cards. They fit import and export buses,
molecular assemblers, inscribers and the ME IO port, and the ExtendedAE and AdvancedAE machines that
accept acceleration cards.
